隐私政策与儿童保护声明

小狮数学乐园（Little Lion Math）

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 9 日 · 版本 1.0.0

《小狮数学乐园》（以下简称"本应用"）由开发者占飞提供。我们深知儿童个人信息保护的重要性，请家长在陪同下仔细阅读本政策。

1我们不收集任何信息

  • 本应用完全离线运行：不申请网络权限，不连接任何服务器，不上传任何数据；
  • 我们不会收集、使用、存储或向第三方共享您或孩子的任何个人信息，包括但不限于姓名、年龄、位置、设备标识符、使用记录；
  • 学习进度（星星、贴纸、关卡记录）仅保存在您的手机本地，卸载应用即彻底删除。

2权限使用说明

权限用途
震动（VIBRATE）儿童答题时的触觉反馈，普通权限，不涉及隐私

本应用不申请网络、定位、相机、麦克风、通讯录、存储、电话等任何其他权限。

3儿童隐私保护

  • 本应用面向 3–6 岁儿童，遵循《中华人民共和国未成年人保护法》《儿童个人信息网络保护规定》及 Google Play 家庭政策；
  • 应用内无广告、无应用内购买、无社交功能、无外部链接跳转；
  • 家长可通过应用内「家长中心」管理设置与数据，该入口设有算术验证以防儿童误操作。

4数据的存储与删除

  • 全部数据通过 Android DataStore 存储于设备本地；
  • 您可随时在「家长中心 → 清空学习数据」一键删除；
  • 卸载应用即删除全部数据；
  • 卸载重装后本地进度无法恢复，属正常设计（无云端账号体系）。

5应用内购买

本节自 V1.1 起生效，当前版本（1.0.0）无任何内购。
  • 提供一次性买断的"解锁全部内容"选项，不含订阅、不含自动续费；
  • 支付由应用市场官方支付渠道完成，我们不接触、不存储任何支付信息；
  • 解锁入口位于「家长中心」，需通过算术验证后进入，防止儿童误操作。

6政策更新

如本政策发生重大变更，我们将在应用更新说明中显著提示。

8儿童个人信息保护政策（专门）

本政策适用于《小狮数学乐园》。本应用面向 3–6 岁儿童，我们深知儿童个人信息的重要性，特制定本政策并采取严格措施保护儿童个人信息安全。

一、是否收集儿童个人信息

不收集。本应用为完全离线的单机应用，不申请网络权限、不具备联网能力；不设注册、登录与账号体系；不含广告、统计、社交等任何第三方 SDK。我们不会以任何方式收集、使用、存储、共享、转让或公开披露儿童及其监护人的任何个人信息。

二、本地数据

学习进度（已通关关卡、获得的星星与贴纸）仅保存在您的设备本地，用于展示学习成果，不会上传至任何服务器，我们无法访问。

三、权限使用

本应用仅申请“震动”权限，用于儿童答题时的触觉反馈，不申请其他任何权限，可在系统设置中随时关闭。

四、数据删除方式

可在「家长中心 → 清空学习数据」一键删除全部本地学习数据，或卸载应用彻底删除全部相关数据。

五、监护人的权利

监护人有权了解、查询、更正、删除儿童个人信息，并可随时撤回同意。由于本应用不收集任何个人信息，上述权利天然得到充分保障。

六、未成年人保护与举报渠道

如发现本应用存在涉及未成年人的不当内容、侵害未成年人合法权益的行为或其他违法违规情形，请通过以下方式举报，我们将在收到举报后及时核实并处理：

  • 举报邮箱：json.zhan@gmail.com
  • 处理时限：收到举报后 15 个工作日内反馈处理结果

七、政策更新

本政策如有变更，将在应用内及本页面同步更新并标注更新日期。更新日期：2026 年 9 月 21 日。

Introduction

Little Lion Math is provided by an independent developer. We take children's privacy seriously and encourage parents to read this policy together with their child.

1We collect nothing

  • The app works fully offline: it does not request internet permission, connects to no server, and uploads nothing.
  • We do not collect, use, store or share any personal information about you or your child, including name, age, location, device identifiers or usage records.
  • Learning progress (stars, stickers, level records) stays on your device only and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

2Permissions

PermissionPurpose
VIBRATEHaptic feedback when answering; a normal permission with no privacy impact

The app requests no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, storage or phone permissions.

3Children's privacy

  • Designed for children aged 3–6 and compliant with COPPA, GDPR-K and the Google Play Families Policy.
  • The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no social features and no external links.
  • A parent area is protected by a simple math problem so children cannot enter it by accident.

4Storage and deletion

  • All data is stored locally on the device via Android DataStore.
  • You can erase everything at any time in Parent Area → Clear learning data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all data.
  • Progress cannot be restored after reinstalling — this is by design, as there is no cloud account.

5In-app purchases

Effective from v1.1. The current version (1.0.0) has no in-app purchases.
  • A one-time "unlock all content" option only — no subscriptions, no auto-renewal.
  • Payments are processed by the official app store; we never see or store payment details.
  • The unlock entry sits inside the parent area behind a math gate.

6Changes

Material changes to this policy will be highlighted in the app's release notes.

8Children's Privacy Policy (Dedicated)

This policy applies to Little Lion Math. The app is designed for children aged 3–6. We recognize the importance of children's personal information and adopt strict measures to protect it.

1. Do we collect children's personal information?

No. The app is fully offline: it does not request internet permission and has no networking capability. There is no registration, login or account system, and no advertising, analytics or social SDKs. We do not collect, use, store, share, transfer or disclose any personal information of children or their guardians.

2. Local data

Progress data (completed levels, stars and stickers) is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server.

3. Permissions

The app requests only the VIBRATE permission for tactile feedback and can be disabled in system settings at any time.

4. Data deletion

Delete all local data via Parents Center → Clear learning data, or by uninstalling the app.

5. Rights of guardians

Guardians have the right to access, correct and delete children's personal information and to withdraw consent at any time.

6. Child safety and reporting channel

If you find any inappropriate content involving minors or any infringement of children's rights, please report it to us. We will verify and handle it promptly:

  • Email: json.zhan@gmail.com
  • Response time: within 15 business days

7. Updates

Any changes will be published in the app and on this page with the update date. Last updated: September 21, 2026.
